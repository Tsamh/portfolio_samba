# This is my portfolio, Samba

**Visit it here: [tsamh.github.io/portfolio_samba](https://tsamh.github.io/portfolio_samba/)**

I built my portfolio to be a time capsule and a playground for my creativity.
Be warned: it's quite the entertainment!

Instead of a plain page of links, it's built to be explored. Here's what you can do there.

## The pages

| Page | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Who I am, my skills, and my CV. |
| **Projects & Certificates** | My projects, sorted by domain (Data, Software, AI Engineering), then my certifications and certificates. |
| **Extra** | Community work, events and giving back. *Coming soon.* |
| **Random** | Random things about me. *Coming soon.* |
| **Contact** | Every way to reach me, and a short message form. |

## Getting around

- **Scroll** to move through a page. Keep scrolling at the bottom and you land
  on the next page. On a phone, swipe.
- **Open the menu** (the button in the middle of the top bar) to jump straight
  to any page.
- A small **"can scroll"** bubble shows up for a moment when there's more
  below or above. Click it to go straight there.
- The thin line on the left shows **how far along the page** you are.
- The browser's **Back button** closes any open window (a photo, a project, the
  CV) and keeps you on the site.

## Things to try

- **Light or dark:** switch the theme with the sun/moon button. Your choice is
  remembered.
- **The CV:** preview it right on the site, choose the plain (ATS) or the
  designed version, and download it.
- **Projects:** pick a domain, then click any project floating around it for
  the full story, the technologies, and links to the code or the live app.
  Certificates open full size with a click.
- **The terminal:** the button at the bottom left (or **Ctrl + Alt + T**) opens a
  small command line to move around the site. Type `help` to start.
- **Hands-free navigation:** the button at the bottom right lets you browse the
  site with hand gestures in front of your webcam. Everything runs in your
  browser: no video is sent, recorded or stored, and the camera turns off as
  soon as you leave.
- **Contact:** fill in the sentence, pick a topic or type your own subject, and
  the message opens ready to send in your own mail app.

## Hidden things

The site has a few **easter eggs**. I won't say where they are. A counter keeps
track of the ones you find, and the terminal can tell you how you're doing.

A tip: don't be afraid of darkness... and getting lost on this site
is not always a bad thing.

## Works everywhere

Desktop, tablet or phone, in any recent browser. Motion is reduced if your
device asks for it.
